# Meshy character and weapon references

Created using built-in image generation, then assembled into three Word documents. The PNG files are the source images for Meshy; the documents organize the views and equipment notes. These are visual references, not finished meshes or guaranteed orthographic geometry.

## Deliverables

- `Spy_Character_Reference.docx`: four body views plus goggles raised and lowered.
- `Merc_Character_Reference.docx`: four body views.
- `Merc_Carbine_Reference.docx`: four weapon views plus first-person framing.

## Prompt set summary

The following records the creative instructions used across generation and correction passes.

### Spy body

Preserve the existing original spy reference: lean build, charcoal cloth suit, olive webbing harness, compact tactical pouches, climbing equipment and flip-up binocular goggles. Produce a consistent neutral A-pose turnaround on a plain gray background, front, anatomical right, back and anatomical left, labeled clearly and at matching scale. Lowered goggles have two small green LED-like optical lights. Add a compact taser and sticky-camera launcher in a slim back holster, with the grip reachable over the right shoulder. Preserve the outfit and gear placement between views. Grounded contemporary materials, no science-fiction armor. Correct the right and left panel ordering without redesigning the character.

### Spy goggles

Use the same spy head and goggle unit in four close-ups: front and right with goggles lowered, front and right with goggles raised. Exactly two green optical lights when lowered; lenses dark when raised. The raised unit must expose the human eye opening with visible eyes, rather than cloth covering the eyes. Preserve the face covering below the eyes and all other headgear.

### Merc body

Preserve the existing original merc reference: broad SWAT silhouette, navy fabric uniform, black plate carrier, helmet and headset, opaque black goggles, fully masked face, gloves, boots and right-thigh holster. Produce matching front, anatomical right, back and anatomical left views in a neutral A-pose, empty hands, plain gray background and consistent scale. Correct side ordering while keeping costume and accessory placement consistent. No exposed face, futuristic armor or science-fiction equipment.

### Merc carbine

Create an original grounded contemporary tactical carbine as a visual reference for a game prop. Dark graphite and black finish, compact optic, handguard flashlight, restrained wear and readable silhouette. Plain gray background, clear right and left profiles plus front and back end views. Keep geometry and accessories visually consistent. No hands or character in the weapon sheet.

### Merc first person framing

Use the same carbine as the weapon reference. Frame it as a first-person game view at lower right, foreshortened and aimed forward toward screen center, with most of the stock outside the frame. Keep optic and flashlight consistent. Plain neutral background, no hands, no character and no environment. This image guides camera composition, while the four-view plate guides modeling.

## Integration notes

Keep the spy goggles, launcher, holster and merc carbine separate from body meshes where possible. The spy's green optical glow needs an emissive material controlled by the goggle state in Unreal. Importing these images or a Meshy model does not implement the weapon actions or animations.
