# Asset provenance

Original character concept sheets were generated with AI as modeling references. Step and shot WAV files were generated procedurally. Game source, map, materials and proxy geometry are included.

Epic mannequin meshes and animation clips imported from MoverExamples are excluded. Recreate them locally with Scripts/migrate_mannequin.py using your Unreal installation, subject to Epic's applicable terms. Unreal Engine is not included. No third-party asset rights are granted by this repository.

This original stealth prototype is unaffiliated with Splinter Cell or Halo. No project-wide open-source license has been selected.
